F FitID

Enter your measurements once.
See your size on every store.

A walkthrough of the FitID extension for Microsoft Edge. Every screenshot below was taken from the real extension running on live stores. Shopping on your phone? Use the website: fitid-sizes.vercel.app.

“A Medium at Zara doesn’t fit the same as a Medium at H&M.” FitID reads each product’s own size chart and matches it to your body, so you stop guessing and returning.

1Install it in Edge (one time)

FitID isn’t in the Edge Add-ons store yet, so you load it from its folder.

  1. Download fitid-extension.zip and unzip it (right-click → Extract All).
  2. Open edge://extensions in a new tab and turn on Developer mode (left sidebar).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped fitid-extension folder.
  4. Pin it: puzzle-piece icon → eye icon next to FitID. The black T-shirt icon stays in your toolbar.

2Create your size profile

The profile page opens by itself after you install. Enter height, chest, waist and shoulders. Height can be in cm or like 5'9. Weight and foot length are optional, and FitID estimates anything you leave blank. Click Save profile.

3Shop as usual. Your size appears by itself.

Open any product on Myntra, AJIO, Flipkart or Amazon. A small FitID card slides in at the bottom right with your size for this exact product, worked out from the product’s own size chart. Nothing to click.

Same profile, other stores and brands

Card buttons: – shrinks it to a small “Your size” pill, × hides it on that page, Edit profile opens your measurements.

4Stores that hide their size guide

Zara, Nykaa Fashion, Levi’s and U.S. Polo only show the chart after you open it. FitID offers to do that for you.

On any other website: open its size guide, click the FitID toolbar icon, then Find my size on this page.

5The toolbar popup: your sizes everywhere

Click the FitID icon in the toolbar at any time.

★Tips

“You’re between M and L”

FitID tells you which way each size goes: the smaller one fits closer, the bigger one is roomier.

Fit preference

On the profile, choose Snug, Regular or Loose. Every recommendation shifts to match.

Add real measurements

Estimated values are labelled. A tape measure on chest, waist and shoulders gives “Great match” results.

On your phone

Extensions only work on laptops and PCs. On mobile use the website: fitid-sizes.vercel.app.
